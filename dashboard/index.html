Sydney Value Buys

No sales data yet. The map, transport, parks, schools, crime and rates tabs work from the reference feeds. Land $/sqm, street tables, drawdowns and betas need the NSW Valuer General sales files:
  1. Open valuation.property.nsw.gov.au - Property Sales Information, pick Yearly, download 2012 to the current year.
  2. Save them as raw\vg\2012.zip ... raw\vg\2026.zip.
  3. Run run_all.cmd in the project folder, then reload this page.

Weights (what matters to you)

Composite = weighted average of Sydney-wide z-scores. Higher = better value for a buyer.

Gross yield and leveraged return by suburb

Houses only. Sorted by gross yield, highest first. Click a column to re-sort, a row to open the suburb.

Gross yield = median weekly house rent for new bonds in the postcode (NSW rental bond lodgements, trailing 12 months) × 52 ÷ median house price. Net yield = gross × (1 − costs). ROE = (net yield + growth − LVR × rate) ÷ (1 − LVR): the first-year return on the equity slice if prices grow at the stated CAGR, before stamp duty, tax and depreciation. Cash ROE uses zero growth. Price and CAGRs come from Valuer General sales where loaded (VG), otherwise the DCJ Rent and Sales Report postcode medians (DCJ, non-strata, 4-quarter average); 10-year CAGR needs VG history.

Sources

Sales
NSW Valuer General Property Sales Information (bulk, weekly/yearly). Contract price, land area, zoning, nature of property. Units (strata) excluded.
Parcels
NSW Spatial Services Land Parcel & Property theme, joined on the Valuer General property ID: parcel centroid and polygon area.
Stations
NSW Spatial Services Features of Interest: train and metro stations (operational).
Schools
NSW Spatial Services Education Facilities: primary, high and combined schools. Quality scores are a hand-maintained file (raw/ref/school_scores.csv) because the HSC / OC league tables block scripted access.
Parks
NSW Planning Portal land zoning: RE1 Public Recreation parcels of 3,000 sqm or more.
Crime
BOCSAR recorded crime by suburb, monthly. Violent = assault, robbery, sexual offences. Property = break and enter dwelling, motor vehicle theft, steal from motor vehicle, steal from dwelling, malicious damage. Per 1,000 residents, 2021 Census (ABS SAL).
Rates
RBA F1.1 cash rate target (monthly average) and F5 bank housing lending rates. ABS Residential Property Price Index (Greater Sydney) to 2021.
Rents
NSW Fair Trading rental bond lodgements, monthly: every new bond with postcode, dwelling type, bedrooms and weekly rent. Trailing 12 months, houses, 10+ bonds per postcode.
Postcode prices
DCJ Rent and Sales Report, sales tables by postcode: quarterly median non-strata sale price from the June 2017 quarter. Used for price and CAGRs until Valuer General sales are loaded.

Definitions

Land $/sqm
Contract price divided by Valuer General land area, houses and vacant residential land only, lots 120 to 5,000 sqm, price at least $150k, $/sqm between 150 and 60,000.
12m medians
Trailing twelve months from the latest contract date in the data (shown in the header). Prior 12 months for the year-on-year change.
Value (ring residual)
Fit ln(median $/sqm) = a + b·km + c·km² across suburbs with 10+ sales, then residual = ln(actual / fitted). Negative residual = cheaper than its distance ring implies. The composite uses minus the residual.
Drawdown
Latest 2-quarter pooled median $/sqm versus the highest such value in 2021-2022. Pooled quarters need 8+ sales.
Beta
OLS slope of the 2-quarter log change in the pooled median on the 2-quarter change in the quarterly-average cash rate, from 2010, needs 16+ overlapping observations. R² shown alongside; treat betas with low R² and few sales as noise.
Composite
Σ weight × z-score ÷ Σ weight, over the components available for that suburb. Z-scores clipped at ±2.5. Distances are log-transformed before scoring.

Caveats

  • Sales appear in the VG file at settlement, so the latest 1 to 3 months are incomplete. Contract date is used throughout.
  • $/sqm ignores the building: a knock-down on a big block and a new build on the same block both count. That is deliberate for a land-value screen, but check zoning (R2 vs R3/R4) and the recent-sales table before reading too much into a street.
  • Suburb polygons and Census names are matched by name; a few suburbs lack population (crime rate blank) or straddle postcodes.
  • Distances are straight-line from the parcel centroid, not walking routes.
  • Nothing here is advice. Public data, personal research.

Refresh

Download the latest Valuer General yearly zip into raw\vg\ (overwrite the current year), then run run_all.cmd. Reference feeds refresh every run. First geocode pass is slow (one request per 400 properties); later runs only look up new IDs.
